# Review: App hosting, tray, overlay, training, UsedBy, inspector, gallery, top level, tools

Read only; nothing changed, nothing run. Line numbers are from commit e98a9d4.

## Map

- **Process entry and the second composition root.** `Program.Main` (Velopack hook → `InstanceStartup.Begin` → `CompositionRoot.Build` → Avalonia). `CompositionRoot` (76 lines) registers diagnostics, `AppState`, clipboard, take-over presenter, then calls six module slices (`EngineModule`, `GesturesModule`, `CommandsModule`, `IgnoredModule`, `TransferModule`, `SyncModule`) and adds the Options/Diagnostics view models inline. `App.axaml.cs` + `App.Launch.cs` (364 lines together) are the runtime half: `StartDesktop` resolves services, wires AppearanceLink, dialog backdrops, Dock, the main-window opener, starts engine and sync, builds the tray with `new`, listens to the single-instance guard and macOS reopen, decides hidden launch.
- **`Hosting/` (42 files) is five subsystems in one folder:** composition modules (7); process identity and launch (`AppInfo`, `AppChannel`, `AppPaths`, `EngineKillSwitch`, `LaunchVisibility`); single instance (10 files around `SingleInstanceGuard` + `InstanceStartup`); start-at-login (`AppState`, `StartupPolicy`, `StartupAction`); engine links (`EngineSettingsLink`, `EnginePauseState`, `StrokeButtonDetection`, `TimerSaveScheduler`, `MainWindowOpener`); and 9 files of sync *runtime* (`SyncService`, `SyncStoreThread`, `SyncFolders`, …) while `Sync/` holds only the dialogs.
- **`Tray/`:** `AppTray` over `ITrayHost` (`AvaloniaTrayHost` / `MacTrayHost`), `ClickDiscriminator`, `TrayIconSet`, `SyncTrayItem`. Clean.
- **`Overlay/`:** two `IStrokeTrail`s (`TrailOverlayWindow` for Windows, `NativeTrailOverlay` over a platform `ITrailSurface` for macOS) sharing `TrailBuffer`, `TrailFrame`, `OverlayStylePolicy`; `TrailCanvas` draws. Invariant 6 is honoured in both (verify-then-show, park between strokes, watchdog, no-op after a bad style).
- **`Training/`:** `TrainingSession` (the state, in App although ADR §5a puts it in Core/Engine), `TrainingRouting` (engine event → session), presenter, window + hand-written XAML view.
- **`UsedBy/`:** the "Used by…" popup, plus the app-wide `ConfirmDialog`/`ConfirmPresenter` and `GestureBindings` (mapping orchestration), which are not "used by" things.
- **`Inspector/`, `DevGallery/`, `Views/`:** small and tidy; `Views/` also holds `ClickAwayFocus` and `MacDockPresence`, which are app behaviours, not views.
- **`tools/`:** `UiShots` re-does `App.StartDesktop`'s AppearanceLink wiring by hand (acknowledged in its comment); `IconTool` is self-contained.
- **Tests:** `tests/Augram.App.Tests` has its own fakes for ports that `Engine.Tests` and `Core.Tests` also fake, and two event-log fakes of its own.

## Findings (most valuable first)

**1. Kind 1. The two trail overlays are one class written twice.** `Overlay/TrailOverlayWindow.cs` and `Overlay/NativeTrailOverlay.cs` share ten fields, the health registration (84–88 / 54–58), `IsUsable`, `FramesRendered`, `Begin`/`Extend`/`End` (104–126 / 82–104), `Dispose` (151–155 / 106–110), `CheckIdle` (158–165 / 113–120), `RequestRender`, first-frame timing and its log line (283–298 / 184–193), and `VerifyOrHide` with the Info/Warning/Error branches and the "not click-through → unusable, End, HideNow" sequence (244–267 / 220–246). Only the surface differs. Recommendation: make `ITrailSurface` the one abstraction (it already is Verify/SetStyle/Cover/SetPoints/Show/Park/Hide, exactly what the window does inside itself): `TrailOverlayWindow` becomes an `ITrailSurface` implementation (styles applied around `base.Show`, DIP maths, `TrailCanvas`), and `NativeTrailOverlay` is renamed the one `StrokeTrail`. `EngineModule` then registers one trail over whichever surface the platform gave. Size L. Risk medium-high: this is invariant 6 code; the window applies styles before *and* after `Show()` and reads back on `Opened`, and its first-frame timing uses `RequestAnimationFrame` while the native one times the hand-off; both test files (`Overlay/TrailOverlayWindowTests`, `NativeTrailOverlayTests`) must keep passing unchanged. Keeps behaviour if those two differences are kept inside the window surface. Must be done by the lead session, never run from a subagent.

**2. Kind 1. Thirteen copies of "find the main window as owner" and six of "show owned, else alone and wait for Closed".** Owner lookup: `App.Launch.cs:57`, `App.axaml.cs:227`, `Transfer/TransferFilePicker.cs:72`, `Import/ImportPresenter.cs:35`, `Transfer/AugramImportWindows.cs:26`, `Hosting/AppClipboard.cs:12`, `Training/TrainingPresenter.cs:38`, `Components/FormDialog/FormDialogPresenter.cs:23`, `UsedBy/ConfirmPresenter.cs:14`, `Components/GesturePicker/GesturePickerPresenter.cs:53`, `UsedBy/UsedByPresenter.cs:46`, `Sync/SyncJoinPresenter.cs:32`, `Sync/SyncConflictPresenter.cs:37`. The show-or-wait sequence: `FormDialogPresenter` 24–34, `AugramImportWindows` 26–37, `TrainingPresenter` 39–48, `UsedByPresenter` 47–56, `GesturePickerPresenter` 54–63, `ConfirmDialog.ShowAloneAsync` 63–71 (and `TakeOverPresenter` + `AugramImportWindows.ShowMessageAsync` are two more ways to show a `ConfirmDialog`). Recommendation: one `Hosting/DialogHost` (or `MainWindowHost`) service: `Owner`, `ShowDialogAsync(window)`, `ShowAsync(window)`, `ShowReplacing(window)` (the "one at a time, close the previous" pattern of Training/UsedBy/GesturePicker, lines 26–36 / 34–44 / 40–51), registered in the root; presenters shrink to building their window. Size M. Risk low-medium: the owned-vs-alone choice matters on a hidden launch and on macOS (README line 28 says presenters "cope with none"); one implementation makes that rule visible. Keeps behaviour.

**3. Kind 2/3. `EngineModule` is four modules, and `App.StartDesktop` is a second composition root.** `Hosting/EngineModule.cs` (338 lines) holds: the config session and stores (59–62, 276–324 incl. the StrokesPlus placeholder upgrade), the platform adapter table (209–274), the Application.Resources service locator for forms (145–164), and the engine proper. The config session is not the engine's: sync, transfer, every tab and a `--no-engine` run need it. Meanwhile `CompositionRoot.cs:57` registers `IButtonCapture` away from its twin `IKeyCapture` (`EngineModule.cs:89`), `App.axaml.cs:202–208` publishes it (its own comment says it "can move beside the key capture"), `MainWindowOpener` is registered inside `RegisterPlatform` (211–212) though it is not an adapter, and `App.axaml.cs:147` does `new AppTray(...)` although README line 5 says nothing outside the root calls `new` on a service. Recommendation: split into `ConfigModule` (session, stores, save scheduler, placeholder upgrade), `PlatformModule` (adapters + null objects), `EngineModule` (host, links, captures, trail), and one `FormServices.Publish(services)` for the three resource keys; register `AppTray` in the root (it can take `MainWindowOpener`/`IAppWindow` for Open and a small quit action); add a `DiagnosticsModule` with its `NavEntry` so Options/Diagnostics stop being the only tabs built inline in `AppNavigation.Build` 40–53. Size M. Risk low-medium: `EngineModuleTests` and `TestAppBuilder` pin the registration surface; disposal order (host before session flush, log last) must survive the split. Keeps behaviour.

**4. Kind 1. Thirteen hand-rolled UI-thread marshals; ADR §5a's "one base class" does not exist.** Default-marshal lambdas `?? (action => Dispatcher.UIThread.Post(action))`: `Hosting/EngineModule.cs:56`, `CompositionRoot.cs:57`, `Hosting/MainWindowOpener.cs:18`, `Tray/SyncTrayItem.cs:26`, `Sync/SyncPrompter.cs:26`, `ViewModels/SyncViewModel.cs:44`. "If on the UI thread run, else post": `Training/TrainingSession.cs:143–150`, `ViewModels/Ignored/IgnoredViewModel.cs:169–175`, `ViewModels/GesturesViewModel.cs:276–282`, `ViewModels/Commands/CommandsViewModel.Projection.cs:28–34`, `Components/Fields/BindingObserver.cs:31–37`, `Components/ItemList/ItemList.cs:176–184`, `Hosting/SyncStoreThread.cs:71–78`. Recommendation: one `Hosting/UiThread` (`Post`, `RunOrPost`, `Invoke`) and one `Action<Action>` marshal registered in the root that every module option defaults to. Size S. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

**5. Kind 1. Each tab module re-registers its siblings' stores and presenters "as fallbacks".** `MappingStore`: `GesturesModule.cs:32`, `CommandsModule.cs:39`, `IgnoredModule.cs:28`. `GestureLibrary`: Gestures 31, Commands 40. `Func<RecognitionOptions>`: Gestures 33, Commands 41. `TrainingSession`/`ITrainingPresenter`: Gestures 34–36 (Add), Commands 42–43 (TryAdd). `IConfirmPresenter`: Gestures 42, Commands 44, Ignored 30. `IFormDialogPresenter`: Commands 46 (Add), Ignored 29, Transfer 24. The comments say "tests, gallery", but the gallery builds its view models with `new` and its own fakes (`DevGallery/CommandGalleryPages.cs:124,143`, `IgnoredGalleryPages.cs:19`), and the module tests register the store themselves (`CommandsModuleTests.cs:28`, `IgnoredModuleTests.cs:23`). The fallbacks hide who owns what and depend on registration order. Recommendation: the stores come from `ConfigModule` (finding 3), the two shared presenters from one `DialogsModule`; the tab modules only resolve. Size S. Risk low (a test that relied on a silent fallback fails loudly and gets an explicit registration). Keeps behaviour.

**6. Kind 1. Five copies of "which platform is this".** `Hosting/AppState.cs:46`, `Hosting/CommandsModule.cs:34`, `Augram.Core/Abstractions/NullWindowOperations.cs:12`, `NullDisplayModes.cs:12`, `Core/Steps/OpenApp/OpenAppStepType.cs:19`. `CommandsModule.CurrentPlatform` is used from `Program`, `IgnoredModule`, `OpenAppStepForm`, `AppMatcherEditViewModel.Picking`, the gallery and tests, so a composition module is the app's platform oracle. Recommendation: `HostPlatforms.Current` in `Core/Abstractions`; the five become one. Size S. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

**7. Kind 1. Two free-name generators and one clash rule.** `ViewModels/Commands/FreeNames.Next` ("stem N", `MappingRules.NameComparer`) and `Training/TrainingSession.NextName` 193–204 ("New gesture N", `GestureRules.NameComparer`) are the same loop; Core's `Mapping/NameScope.Claim` is the "(2)" rule. The importer's `UniqueName` was the same miss. Recommendation: move `FreeNames.Next` to Core beside `NameScope` with the comparer as a parameter; `TrainingSession` calls it. Size S. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

**8. Kind 1/5. Four argument parsers, two of which read the process args themselves.** `Hosting/AppPaths.cs:14` and `Hosting/EngineKillSwitch.cs:14` call `Environment.GetCommandLineArgs()`; `LaunchVisibility` gets args passed (`Program.cs:25`, `App.axaml.cs:98`); `--gallery` is matched inline at `App.axaml.cs:112`. An agent cannot find the app's switches in one place, and the first two cannot be given test args at the root. Recommendation: one `Hosting/LaunchArguments` record parsed once in `Program.Main` and registered (`ConfigFolder`, `NoEngine`, `Hidden`, `Gallery`), each old static becoming a one-line reader of it. Size S. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

**9. Kind 1. Three one-shot timer abstractions.** `Hosting/TimerSaveScheduler` (fixed delay + marshal), `SyncService.cs:46,90` (`Func<TimeSpan, Action, IDisposable>`, defaulting to a *new* `TimerSaveScheduler` per call), and `Hosting/StrokeButtonDetection.cs:58` building its own `System.Threading.Timer` and marshalling by hand. Recommendation: one `Hosting/OneShotTimer` (`Schedule(delay, action)`, marshal injected) used by config save, sync and stroke detection; `TimerSaveScheduler` becomes it. Size S. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

**10. Kind 3. `TrainingSession` is in App though ADR §5a says training is a Core/Engine concern.** `Training/TrainingSession.cs` uses only Core types except `Dispatcher.UIThread` (143–150) and `Components/GestureDrawArea/ScreenArea` (a pure record). `TrainingRouting` (CapturePoint → GesturePoint, 26–35) is the engine-event adapter. Recommendation: move `TrainingSession`, `ITrainingSession`, `TrainingRequest`, `TrainingOutcome` and `ScreenArea` to `Core/Training/` with the marshal injected (finding 4), and `TrainingRouting` to `Engine` beside `EnginePorts.Intercept`; the App keeps presenter, window, view. Size M. Risk low-medium: `TrainingSessionTests` move with it; the gallery and `TrainingWindow()`'s design-time constructor build a session by hand. Keeps behaviour.

**11. Kind 2. `AppState` does two jobs.** `Hosting/AppState.cs` is a projection of three settings for the tray/Options (51–94) and the start-at-login reconciler with its own log source, exception policy and note (96–215). Recommendation: `StartAtLoginLink` (shaped like `EngineSettingsLink`: follows the store, talks to `IStartupRegistration`, owns `StartAtLoginNote`), leaving `AppState` a plain projection. Size M. Risk low-medium (`AppStateTests`, 329 lines, split along the same line). Keeps behaviour.

**12. Kind 2. `SingleInstanceGuard` is a listener and a client in one file.** Server side (`Listen`, `CreateServer`, `ServeAsync`, `ReadRequestAsync`, `AnswerAsync`, 200–322) and client side (`Send`, `SignalExisting`, 93–161) share only the two name helpers (181–196). Recommendation: `InstanceListener` (mutex + pipe, the events) and a static `InstancePeer`/`InstanceClient`, with `InstanceStartup` the only caller of both; the 10 instance files get their own `Hosting/Instance/` folder and README. Size M. Risk low (tests use real pipes under unique names and would move with the types). Keeps behaviour.

**13. Kind 1. Five hand-built dialog shells and four identical Escape handlers.** DockPanel "dialog" + StackPanel "dialog-buttons" assembled by hand in `UsedBy/ConfirmDialog.cs:33–53`, `UsedBy/UsedByWindow.cs:53–69`, `Sync/SyncConflictWindow.cs:35–55`, `Transfer/AugramImportView.cs:25–44` and `Training/TrainingView.axaml`; `OnKeyDown` Escape-closes in `ConfirmDialog` 73–83, `UsedByWindow` 94–104, `SyncConflictWindow` 70, `AugramImportWindow` 33. Recommendation: a lookless `Components/DialogShell` (title + ⓘ, content, buttons, Escape) that `FormDialogWindow` and these five host; `ConfirmDialog` could even be a `FormDialogRequest` with a `NoteField`. Size M. Risk low. Keeps behaviour.

## What the READMEs for these folders should cover (map gaps)

- **`Hosting/README.md`** (or one per sub-folder after finding 17): the startup order as one numbered list (`Program.Main` → Velopack → `InstanceStartup.Begin` → `CompositionRoot.Build` → `App.Initialize` → `OnFrameworkInitializationCompleted` → `ChooseThenStart`/`StartDesktop` → `EngineModule.Start` → `SyncModule.Start` → tray → `HandOver`/`OpenAsLaunched`), and the shutdown order in reverse; which module registers what and in which order, and why (today only comments in `CompositionRoot` say it); the threads that App introduces (`augram-single-instance`, `augram-instance-mutex`, `augram-sync`, the save timer) as a table like Engine's; the switches (`--hidden`, `--no-engine`, `--config-folder`, `--gallery`, `AUGRAM_NO_ENGINE`); "settings → engine" links and who may write the store (today a paragraph in the big README); the Application.Resources keys forms look up and why.
- **`Overlay/README.md`:** invariant 6 restated with the exact sequence per stroke for each surface, parked vs hidden, the watchdog, the no-op-for-the-run rule, units (physical pixels vs points), and the sentence "never run this from a subagent".
- **`Tray/README.md`:** `ITrayHost` contract, click reconstruction and the 300 ms preview, menu order, tooltip grammar, icon assets and `tools/IconTool`.
- **`Training/README.md`:** the session's state machine (Begin/Replace/Accept/Cancel), the two stroke sources and which thread each arrives on, `PublishCanvas` routing, and the ADR note that it is slated for Core.
- **`UsedBy/` → `Dialogs/README.md`:** which presenter to use for what (confirm, form dialog, picker, take-over, message), the owner rule (owned when the main window is visible, alone otherwise, never on a hidden launch), and the one-at-a-time replacement rule.
- **`DevGallery/README.md`:** the gallery rule, `ThemePair`, where fakes live, how a page is declared and how `UiShots --gallery` renders it.
- **`Inspector/README.md`:** `Region.Mark` contract, who marks (renderers), the report format.
- **`Views/README.md`:** `MainWindow` composes only; the behaviours (`ClickAwayFocus`, `MacDockPresence`) and why they are not components.
- **`tools/README.md`:** one table of the tools, when to run them, what they may and may not touch on Joel's machine.

Where an agent arriving cold gets lost today: the start-up order lives in three files (`Program`, `App.axaml.cs`, `App.Launch.cs`) plus seven module `Register`/`Start` pairs, with the sequence only in the prose of a 226-line README; the Hosting folder mixes composition, launch, instance, startup, engine links and sync; "where do I show a dialog" has thirteen answers; and the `Exclusions`/`Ignored` wording split is documented but still costs a search each time.
