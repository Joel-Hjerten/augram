Augram glass theme

A first pass at the new look for Augram's settings window: frosted glass, layers that get lighter as they come forward, and rounded tabs. Click around inside the window; the switches change what the real app would offer, or what it would meet on each system.

Mockup only: the controls respond, nothing is saved. Window corners follow the system (8 px on Windows 11); the Corners slider sets the panels inside. Wallpaper tint stands in for Windows 11's Mica, which picks up only the wallpaper's colour; frosted glass blurs whatever is behind the window, as Acrylic and the macOS blur do.

What to look at

  • Four layers, each a step lighter: the window glass, the tab's page, the panels, the controls. No outlines; panels have a faint light edge instead.
  • The top tab is joined to its page. Sub-tabs are pills inside the page, so the two levels never look alike.
  • The accent follows the trail colour (Options › Appearance › Trail) unless Accent colour is switched off. Each theme gets a lighter or darker shade of it so text stays readable; try Green in Light.
  • Switches for a single on/off on its own row (Start at login, an excluded app's Active). Check boxes in list rows and where you tick several side by side (Ignore keys, Use on).
  • Daylight with Dark and a low tint shows where the text stops being readable. Solid is what Windows falls back to when transparency is off.
  • Options › Appearance holds the user's own controls for all of this: theme, window background, tint and corner rounding (12 px by default). The switches above the window are the same settings.

Questions for you

  • With the accent following the trail, a fresh install would be green, the StrokesPlus default. Make the icon's gold the fresh-install trail colour instead?
  • Section titles: flip Options titles above the window. Panels on the other tabs keep their title inside, next to their buttons; Options has no buttons in its headers, so either works there.
